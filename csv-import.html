Import games from CSV

Bring your Commander game history from Excel, Google Sheets, or another spreadsheet into your Ledger.

1. Prepare your CSV

Export your spreadsheet as a .csv file. Each row should represent one game. Date, Deck (or Commander), and Result (or Winner) are required.

Date

Use 2026-11-01 or 11/01/2026.

Deck

Use a deck name or commander matching one of your imported decks.

Result

Use Win or Loss. Yes/No and 1/0 also work.

Optional columns: Commander, Player, Opponent Commanders, Opponent Names, First Player, Winner, and Notes. Any CSV column containing “Opponent” is scanned for commanders, including Opponent 1, Opponent 2, and Opponent Commander 3. Separate multiple commanders in a cell with semicolons. For First Player and Winner, use a player name, commander, or seat number (1 is you). We also recognize common alternatives like “Game Date,” “My Commander,” and “Outcome.”

2. Upload and validate

We'll find the closest matching deck or commander, even with spelling mistakes. Review suggested matches or choose Create new deck, then check errors and duplicates before importing. Nothing is saved, including new decks, until you confirm.

Choose a CSV file, then click Validate selected CSV.

Optional: checks unique opponent commander names in small batches (maximum 25 per click). Suggestions are not applied automatically. This does not connect opponents to user accounts or infer that separate CSV rows are the same game.

3. Import valid games

Only rows marked Ready will be imported. Any new decks you select will be created at that time. You can fix skipped rows in your spreadsheet and upload again.
