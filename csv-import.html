THE COMMANDER ARCHIVE · MATCH RECORDS

Import games from CSV

Bring your Commander game history from Excel, Google Sheets, or another spreadsheet into your Ledger.

1. Prepare your CSV

Export your spreadsheet as a .csv file. Each row should represent one game. Only three columns are required.

Date

Use 2026-11-01 or 11/01/2026.

Deck

Use a deck name or commander matching one of your imported decks.

Result

Use Win or Loss. Yes/No and 1/0 also work.

Optional columns: Opponents (separate commanders with semicolons) and Notes. We also recognize common alternatives like “Game Date,” “My Commander,” and “Outcome.”

2. Upload and validate

We'll find the closest matching deck or commander, even with spelling mistakes. Review suggested matches, then check errors and duplicates before importing. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

3. Import valid games

Only rows marked Ready will be imported. You can fix skipped rows in your spreadsheet and upload again.
